Politics
Victoria votes as Labor faces a heavy defeat
Election day has arrived in Victoria, with about half of the state's 4.7 million voters already having voted early or by post. The final Newspoll (20-25 November) had the Coalition on 33% of the primary vote, Labor 20%, One Nation 18% and the Greens 17%, with the Coalition ahead 57.5 to 42.5 after preferences. Labor, now led by Ben Carroll after Jacinta Allan resigned in July, has been hurt by Big Build corruption allegations and state debt. Liberal leader Jess Wilson may form government with a historically low primary vote, and One Nation is expected to win more than 20%. ABC election coverage starts at 6pm AEDT.
Why it matters to you: A big swing against Labor in Victoria is a warning sign about cost-of-living anger that federal Labor will also face.

Schools push back on Google's AI search summaries
Los Angeles school officials have quietly disabled Google's AI Overview summaries on school-issued devices. Google says search and AI are deeply integrated and told The New York Times that if schools ban AI, students will not have access to search. New York and Los Angeles announced one-year moratoriums on student use of generative AI in September, and hundreds of Google employees have signed a petition asking for a way to turn AI features off without losing search.

Health
New drug roughly doubles survival in advanced pancreatic cancer
The US FDA has approved daraxonrasib for metastatic pancreatic cancer in patients whose disease has progressed on at least one earlier treatment. In the trial, median overall survival was 13.2 months versus 6.7 months on chemotherapy, and the European Commission has also approved it. It is not a cure, but it is the first of a new class of targeted RAS-blocking drugs for one of the deadliest cancers.
Why it matters to you: A real step forward for a cancer with few options, and a sign of how fast targeted drugs are moving from trial to patients.
Sources: Memorial Sloan Kettering · CancerNetwork
