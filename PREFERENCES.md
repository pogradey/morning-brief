## Listener
- 20 years old, lives in Sydney, Australia. Listens in the car, so audio only.
- Wants to be well informed about what actually matters. No filler.

## Defaults
- Length: 4 to 5 minutes. Never longer than 5.
- Style: fast, with one line of why it matters to him after each story.
- Topics, in rough priority:
  1. Finance, markets and the economy (Australia first, then US and global)
  2. AI and technology
  3. Health and medical science
  4. Politics (Australian federal and NSW, then US and world)
  5. Big world news
- Leave out: celebrity, sport results, crime-of-the-day, viral clips, lifestyle tips, product launches that are just ads.
- Voice: en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural at +8% speed (set in tools/make_episode.py).
